## Targeted copyedits

- Remove the slide-like `Plan:` and `Next step:` labels without weakening the recommendation or command.
- Replace the decorative dash with ordinary sentence structure while preserving the explicit `not because` relationship.
- Preserve functional punctuation and formatting in the time, ratio, command, channel, and lexical hyphen. Do not replace them merely to lower a symbol count.

## Assertions

- **Fidelity:** Keep the 10% recommendation, the explicit statement that the current build is not broken, the need for one more follow-up check, the complete next step, the stated writing goal, and the instruction not to guess.
- **Naturalness:** Remove only the decorative label frames and dash scaffolding. The result should read like an ordinary teammate wrote it rather than a formatted summary.
- **Register:** Keep the message concise, direct, and ready to send at work.
- **Voice preservation:** Retain the restrained contrast, the plain statement about clarity, and the firm final reminder.
- **Non-invention:** Add no rollout result, diagnosis, owner, deadline, sample, deployment outcome, or reason beyond the source.

## Common failures

- Turn the statement about the build not being broken into a new causal claim.
- Remove or alter `--dry-run`, `10:30`, `3:1`, `#release-ops`, or the lexical hyphen in `follow-up`.
- Treat every colon, hyphen, dash, or backtick as forbidden punctuation.
- Add a heading, bullet list, summary label, or editorial note.
