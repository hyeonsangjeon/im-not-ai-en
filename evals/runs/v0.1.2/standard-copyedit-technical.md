The parser allocates a worker to each batch. Both the request ID and the trace ID are copied into `job_meta`. We observed 12 retries during a 30-minute soak test. The parser sends the normalized record to the scheduler, which stores the timestamp. In this path, the scheduler, not the parser, owns that timestamp. On the fallback path, the worker caps the delay at 800 ms. The cap depends on `retry_budget_ms`; it does not replace `Retry-After`. The two limits are not equivalent.
